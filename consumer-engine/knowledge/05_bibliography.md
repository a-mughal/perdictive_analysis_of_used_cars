# 05 — Bibliography (the "books")

The knowledge files summarise these works in the engine's own words. Agents may cite the tag,
never reproduce the text. Add to this list when you add a framework.

## Consumer psychology & decision-making
- Kahneman, D. — *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011). System 1/2, loss aversion, anchoring, WYSIATI, peak-end.
- Ariely, D. — *Predictably Irrational* (2008). Relativity, zero price, endowment, pain of paying.
- Cialdini, R. — *Influence* (1984; rev. 2021). Reciprocity, social proof, authority, scarcity, consistency.
- Thaler, R. & Sunstein, C. — *Nudge* (2008). Defaults, choice architecture.
- Schwartz, B. — *The Paradox of Choice* (2004). Option overload.
- Sutherland, R. — *Alchemy* (2019). Costly signalling, satisficing, psycho-logic.
- Lindstrom, M. — *Buyology* (2008). Sensory and unconscious drivers.

## Shopping behaviour
- Underhill, P. — *Why We Buy* (1999; rev. 2008). Interception, conversion, friction, orientation.
- Sharp, B. — *How Brands Grow* (2010). Mental/physical availability, double jeopardy.

## Needs & jobs
- Christensen, C. et al. — *Competing Against Luck* (2016). Jobs to be done.
- Ulwick, A. — *What Customers Want* (2005). Outcome-driven innovation, opportunity scoring.
- Kano, N. et al. — "Attractive quality and must-be quality" (1984). Kano model.
- Fitzpatrick, R. — *The Mom Test* (2013). How to ask customers without being lied to.

## Positioning & adoption
- Dunford, A. — *Obviously Awesome* (2019). Competitive alternatives, best-fit customer.
- Moore, G. — *Crossing the Chasm* (1991). Early adopters vs early majority.
- Rogers, E. — *Diffusion of Innovations* (1962). Adopter categories.

## Innovation method
- Altshuller, G. — TRIZ / *The Innovation Algorithm*. 40 inventive principles.
- Kim, W.C. & Mauborgne, R. — *Blue Ocean Strategy* (2005). ERRC grid.
- Christensen, C. — *The Innovator's Dilemma* (1997). Disruption from below.
- Ries, E. — *The Lean Startup* (2011). Build-measure-learn; MVP.

## Forecasting & risk
- Kahneman & Tversky — planning fallacy; reference-class forecasting (Flyvbjerg, 2006).
- Klein, G. — "Performing a Project Premortem", HBR (2007).
- Tetlock, P. & Gardner, D. — *Superforecasting* (2015). Calibration, Brier scores.
- Hubbard, D. — *How to Measure Anything* (2007). Calibrated estimation, Monte-Carlo.
